that requires changing a ROM is assigned to v1.3.8 rather than replacing these

Former delta descriptions belonged to invalidated candidates and are not a
valid size claim for the current source. The current implementation replaces the old
target-level refill wrapper with a one-shot fixed-raw-EXP wrapper, updates the
live-join visibility guard that arms it, and places the bounded Scenario 10
ownership helper at `0x31E380`. The visibility guard now treats the persisted
active scenario at `0xA49C` as authoritative during ordinary play and both
cold and warm title-screen LOAD. It reads the next-scenario scratch at
`0xA612` only when the scheduler stack proves that the outer callback is the
stock result scanner at `0x00CEC4`; a stale nonzero selector can no longer
override a loaded save.

The fixed raw grants are Keith `0x00`, Lester `0x90`, and Jessica `0x60`.
Each value reconstructs `(original tier-2 level - 1) * original class gauge`,
excluding the Japanese row's residual EXP bar.  The grant is identical across
Original, Normal, and Hard and across all three selected branches; the selected
class's own gauge determines the resulting LV/EXP, with no Hard-only addition.
Only the new one-shot `0xA5` live-join marker authorizes a grant.  A legacy
v1.3.6 `0x5A` marker is cleared without a grant, and a later Rune Stone restart
has no marker and therefore keeps the stock `LV1/EXP0` result.

## Completed sequential runtime acceptance

The completed route used isolated Xvfb displays, empty runtime homes and fresh
SRAM on the exact hashes above. Every step executed the real victory result,
experience/join/event processing and save menu; each produced save fed the next
route step. Combat was shortened with current victory-near probe ROMs, so this
is not a claim that 31 full battles were manually played from their first turn.

### Scenario 12 first-turn RNG boundary

On the superseded pre-cold-load-fallback candidate, Original (`Pure`) and
Normal reached identical runtime groups; the observed WRAM difference was one
VBlank at the LCG seed boundary. Original reproduced both the natural `HP0`
defeat endpoint and turn 2 with `HP1`; Normal and Hard also reached the natural
`HP0` endpoint. Those captures belong to invalidated ROM hashes and are not
final acceptance evidence for the current candidate.

Accordingly, the inspection policy accepts either of the two normal Scenario 12
endpoints—turn 2 or a natural `HP0` defeat—when it is backed by the required
artifact hashes for each of Original, Normal and Hard. This is an RNG-boundary
acceptance rule, not a waiver of the three-profile check. The exact-ROM
sequential route has completed; the broader 612-check post-release audit is
still running under the v1.3.8-if-fixed policy described below.

- Recheck Original, Normal and Hard Scenario 1-through-ending sequential play,
  including save/result transitions and alternate scenarios, in the broader
  post-release audit.
- Natural joins for Keith (Scenario 7), Lester (Scenario 10) and Jessica
  (Scenario 11), with Lester's default and Croco Lord selection paths.

- Rune Stone restart for Keith, Lester and Jessica from tiers 2, 3, 4 and 5.
